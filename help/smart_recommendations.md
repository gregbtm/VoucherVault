# Smart Recommendations

VoucherVault can automatically suggest items that need attention based on their status, expiry date, or balance.

## How Recommendations Work

Smart recommendations are generated automatically for items matching these criteria:

### Expiry Soon
Items expiring within **7 days** receive an expiry reminder. Items expiring within **1 day** get a final urgent reminder.

### Very Low Balance
Gift cards or vouchers with a remaining balance **under £5** are flagged for quick use or consolidation.

### Long Unused
Items you haven't used or checked in over **6 months** may be forgotten. This is especially useful for identifying gift cards that might expire or subscriptions you've forgotten about.

## Viewing Recommendations

1. Open **Navigation → Recommendations** (or click the notifications bell icon)
2. See all active suggestions ranked by priority
3. Click any recommendation to view the item
4. Click **Dismiss** to hide a suggestion (you can re-enable it later)

## Dismissing & Re-enabling Recommendations

- **Dismiss:** Click the **Dismiss** button next to any recommendation. The item is still tracked but won't appear in suggestions.
- **Re-enable:** Go to your **Preferences** and toggle "Show dismissed recommendations" to see them again.

## Why No Recommendations?

If you don't see any recommendations, it means:
- All your items are in good standing (not expiring soon, good balance, recently used)
- You've dismissed all suggestions
- The feature is disabled in your Preferences

## Tips

- **Weekly Check:** Review recommendations weekly to stay on top of expiring items
- **Budget Planning:** Use low-balance recommendations to plan which items to use next
- **Consolidation:** Group low-balance items by merchant to see consolidation opportunities
- **Travel:** Check recommendations before trips to identify travel passes expiring soon
