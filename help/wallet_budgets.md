# Wallet Budgets

Set monthly spending limits on wallets to control gift card and voucher spending.

## Setting a Budget

1. Go to **Navigation → Manage Wallets**
2. Click on any wallet
3. Scroll to **Budget** section
4. Click **Set Monthly Limit**
5. Enter your limit (e.g., £100)
6. Save

## How Budgets Work

- **Monthly reset:** Budget resets automatically on the 1st of each month
- **Spent tracking:** The system totals all transactions in items within this wallet
- **Visual indicator:** A progress bar shows your spending vs. limit
- **Alerts:** When you exceed 80% of your limit, an alert appears

## Spending Calculation

Budget spent = sum of all transaction **values** for items in the wallet.

For example:
- Wallet: "Subscriptions"
- Items: Netflix (£10), Spotify (£5), Gym (£15)
- If you've marked 2 transactions against these items (using Netflix, used Gym), spent = £25
- Monthly limit: £50
- Usage: 50% (£25 / £50)

## Multiple Items, One Budget

A budget applies to an entire wallet. If your wallet contains multiple gift cards or vouchers, all spending counts toward the single budget limit.

**Example:**
- Wallet: "Dining Out"
- Items: Wagamama, Pizza Express, Deliveroo
- Budget: £200/month
- Any transactions across these three items count toward your £200 limit

## Tips

- **Per-category budgets:** Create separate wallets for major spending categories (Subscriptions, Dining, Shopping)
- **Household budgets:** Share wallets with family and set group budgets
- **Track spending:** Use budgets to control discretionary spending
- **Plan ahead:** Set budgets before the month starts to avoid overspending

## Viewing Budget History

Budget spent is recalculated daily. The current month's total is always visible on the wallet page. Previous months' data is not stored (reset happens automatically).

## Disabling a Budget

To remove a budget:
1. Go to **Manage Wallets**
2. Click the wallet
3. Click **Remove Budget**
4. Confirm

Removing a budget does not delete any items or transactions — it just stops tracking the spending limit.
